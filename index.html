The garden gives leaves. We give wings.

From the Head Master

How we work

Three things we do differently

No child drifts away

We come to your home

When a child becomes irregular, the Head Master visits the family himself. Where a visit is difficult, a card goes directly to the parents. No child is allowed to quietly stop coming.

Learning by doing

The kitchen garden

A low-cost greenhouse built with help from parents and the community. Children grow their own vegetables and learn practical skills that outlast any textbook.

A door worth opening

Free JNV coaching

Extra classes after school and in the holidays, plus free guide books, so children from tea garden families get a real chance at a Jawahar Navodaya Vidyalaya seat.

Finding us

Come through the gate

The school stands just off the road at Rajoi. You will find it easily.

You do not need an appointment to visit. Any school morning, walk in and ask for the Head Master.

    From the school's own UDISE+ returns

    Sixteen years of children on the roll

    The roll rose to 199 in 2012-13, fell to 126 in 2016-17, and has been climbing back ever since. Since 2021-22 it has held steadily between 164 and 174 children in Classes I–V.

    Children in Classes I–V, 2010-11 to 2025-26

    What the numbers show

    Turned around

    From 126 children at the low point in 2016-17 to 174 today — a rise of 38% in nine years.

    Steady five years

    164 to 174 children every year since 2021-22, which makes planning meals, books and teaching far easier.

    Senior classes full

    Classes IV and V together hold 77 children, the most since 2014-15. Older children are staying on.

    Girls nearly half

    Girls were about 45% of Classes I–V in the first eight years on record, and close to 49% in the last eight.

    Strong pre-primary

    Pre-primary reached a 16-year high of 48 children in 2024-25 and stands at 36 this year — next year's Class I.

    Consistently full

    In 12 of the last 16 years the Class I–V roll has been 150 children or more.
